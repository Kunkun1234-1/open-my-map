# Default photo covers

Maps and projects share a pool of 20 photos. Four are the existing [workspace city photos](../workspace/SOURCES.md): Shenzhen Bay, Suzhou Shantang Road, Qingdao Old Town, and Nanjing Qixia Mountain. Four are existing repository images: `cover-shenzhen.webp`, `cover-island.webp`, `cover-lake.webp`, and `cover-shanghai.webp`.

The other 12 were generated individually with the built-in ImageGen tool. Each prompt used this shared direction:

> Generate one standalone realistic photograph for a default cover in Open My Map, an editorial geography and travel workspace.
> Landscape composition suited for a 5:3 center crop, keep the key subject in the middle 65% vertically, edge-to-edge photo. High-quality editorial visual, natural realistic surfaces, no borders, no titles, no logos, no maps, no pins, no collage, no route overlays.

Each photo also used the following scene description:

- `chengdu-teahouse.webp` — A quiet old Chengdu teahouse courtyard in late afternoon, worn bamboo chairs and small dark-wood tables with white gaiwan tea bowls, one seated patron in the middle distance, green bamboo and old timber doors, steam catching soft backlight. Warm restrained analog film, olive and tobacco colors, human and authentic, framed at seated eye level.
- `chengdu-night-market.webp` — A Chengdu street-food night market just after a light rain, an open late-night stall with a vendor and rising steam under amber incandescent bulbs, soft silhouettes, reflections on wet paving. Intimate Chinese street photography, warm reds and deep ink shadows, subtle 35mm grain. Avoid large signs and legible text.
- `desert-road.webp` — A single empty road winding through a vast ochre desert with sandstone folds at sunset. Low viewpoint, elegant soft layers and very little visual clutter. Editorial road-trip photography, earthy sienna and apricot, restrained color.
- `misty-forest.webp` — A quiet wet evergreen forest trail in morning mist, ferns and moss around a winding footpath with filtered light. Deep greens, warm gray fog, intimate film photography with natural shadows and credible detail.
- `snow-village.webp` — A small snow-covered alpine village at blue hour, a few warmly lit windows, stone houses and distant mountains receding into haze. Quiet winter travel photography, soft cool tones and warm points of light, no marked routes.
- `museum-concrete.webp` — An architectural editorial photograph of a brutalist concrete museum courtyard, sunlight crossing a broad stair, one distant visitor for scale, muted warm gray and muted olive. Spare geometric composition, natural photographic perspective, no text.
- `rainy-bicycles.webp` — Quiet urban street in rain, parked bicycles by an old corner bookstore window, soft reflections and a passerby with umbrella in the distance. Rich black-and-white 35mm documentary photograph with soft silvery light, no readable typography.
- `coastal-cliffs.webp` — Windswept coastal cliffs and a small lighthouse above blue-gray water, seen from a grassy footpath, soft cloud light, motion in the grass, slate and sage palette. Natural understated geographic editorial photograph.
- `botanical-house.webp` — A lush botanical glasshouse with weathered glass panes, tall palms and a single centered pathway, dappled spring sunlight. Medium-format botanical editorial photograph, warm greens and faded ivory highlights, architectural details.
- `rice-terraces.webp` — Rice terraces in southwest China at dawn, ribbons of reflected sky in water and soft green slopes with a tiny farmhouse in mist. Elevated view like a travel photograph, organic repeating curves, pale teal and straw tones, no labels or drawn overlays.
- `train-window.webp` — A quiet railway carriage window framing a late-afternoon landscape of hills and warm fields, hint of a notebook resting on a wood ledge, no readable text. Nostalgic documentary photograph, layered window reflection, faded copper and grass green.
- `dawn-balloons.webp` — A wide dawn photograph of a few distant hot-air balloons over a soft, rolling rural valley, low mist in the folds, grass in the foreground and early pale rose light. Understated medium-format travel editorial photograph with balanced negative space and a crisp natural landscape, no oversaturation.
