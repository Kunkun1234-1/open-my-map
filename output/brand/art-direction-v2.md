## A：开放的岛

不规则墨黑轮廓代表个人地图，缺口表达开放，朱红圆点代表自己选择的地点。暖白底、衬线字标。

### 提示词 A

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a new artistic logo concept for the personal map-making project "Open My Map".
User feedback: previous blue folded-map letter M was unattractive and felt like an ordinary SaaS icon, lacking artistic design. Start over completely; do not preserve that logo. This time make an original artful emblem with a strong memorable silhouette, closer to a contemporary art museum, independent cartography journal or architectural studio identity. Sophisticated and restrained, not corporate tech branding.

Design concept: "a place left open". One bold, asymmetrical, irregular black loop like a simplified abstract island contour drawn in one confident thick ink gesture. The loop is deliberately open in one place, allowing a narrow curved route of negative space to enter toward the center. A single small vermilion red solid circular dot sits just off-center in the empty interior, representing a personally chosen place. The symbol should have the calm spatial intelligence of abstract sculpture and the human touch of hand-cut paper. Subtle organic tension, beautiful interplay of solid and empty, asymmetrical yet optically balanced. Around 2-3 distinctive turns in the outside contour only; extremely reduced and iconic. Avoid a perfect circle or a recognizable letter. The contour must feel designed and distinctive, not a random scribble. Clean smooth edges with an intentionally organic shape. Flat solid ink black and one flat vermilion red dot only, no texture, no gradients.

Presentation: a single exquisite logo on a warm pale ivory background in a landscape 3:2 canvas. Large centered emblem, with broad intentional empty space surrounding it. Below the emblem place the exact brand name "Open My Map" in small beautifully spaced black editorial serif type. The emblem is the clear hero, wordmark restrained. Only one logo, no grid, no multiple variations, no board labels, no explanations, no mockups. The result should be arresting, poetic, understated and visually sophisticated. A real usable brand mark rather than an illustration.

Avoid: folded map, letter M monogram, generic location pin, globe, compass, map clipart, Google colors, blue-purple gradients, rounded-square app tile, symmetrical corporate geometry, swooshes, cartoon style, busy topographic lines, ornamental detail, distressed ink textures, shadows, 3D effects, photographs, watermarks, slogans.

## B：地图之窗

群青与朱红交错的开放轮廓，以地图图层和空间负形表达探索。

文件：`open-my-map-art-atlas-v2.png`。PNG 概念稿，未接入网站，未矢量化。

### 提示词 B

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a second, completely different, exceptionally artistic logo direction for the custom cartography project "Open My Map". This is an art-directed identity, akin to an experimental architecture gallery or independent design biennale. The user rejected generic blue folded-map M icons as lacking artistic design. We need conceptual invention and precise beautiful graphic form.

Single concept: "the atlas becomes a window". Construct a compact abstract emblem from three bold nested irregular rectangular contour bands, like simplified topographic contours or die-cut map pages seen straight on, each with an intentional open interruption. The bands flow around a shared off-center ivory negative-space passage, creating a deep spatial opening without any gradients, shadows, 3D or perspective rendering. Hard geometry meets just one unexpected smooth river-like inward curve. Strong harmonious graphic rhythm. Each band is a broad flat solid shape. The OUTER band is rich ultramarine cobalt blue, the MIDDLE band is vivid burnt vermilion orange, the INNER band is ultramarine blue again. The silhouette should be around square, gently irregular, elegant as a typographic glyph, truly simple and recognizable. Three bands only, no intricate nested lines. Interlocking but open, with exact sophisticated negative-space cuts. Think bold screenprinted avant-garde cartographic abstraction, not a standard icon. A spatial symbol that suggests layers, routes, discovery. No literal map, no letter M, no globe, no pin, no compass. No circle with a dot.

Layout: one single large centered emblem above a small restrained precisely typeset wordmark "open my map" in lowercase black modern editorial sans serif, slightly spaced. Warm ivory plain background, landscape 3:2 canvas, huge calm margins. Flat vector-like artwork, sharp clean edges, no textures, no gloss, no mockup, no board, no extra text, no watermarks. Colors are deliberate striking solid ultramarine and vermilion with ivory negative space. The result must be compositionally beautiful, original and quietly daring, artful not cute, suitable for a mature creative mapping product.
