## 01 开口字标

文件：`01-editorial-wordmark.png`

### Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Original identity exploration for "Open My Map", a creative web product where people make, curate and share personal maps. User requests strong artistic design, premium restraint, creativity and simplicity; has rejected a generic blue folded-map M and wants substantially DIFFERENT directions. Design with the taste and formal precision of an excellent independent graphic design studio. An effective logo, not a poster illustration. Exact brand spelling as specified below. One concept only.
Presentation: landscape 3:2 canvas, clean warm ivory background, one optically centered logo lockup, generous margins. Flat vector-like sharp artwork, refined proportions, impeccable kerning. Do NOT create multiple versions, grids, mockups, labels, explanation paragraphs or decorative props. NO shadows, gradients, textures, gloss, 3D, generic location pins, globes, blue-purple tech branding, folded map M. Logo should work in one or two solid inks and at small sizes.
TYPOGRAPHIC DIRECTION — no separate pictorial symbol. Design a stunning bespoke lowercase wordmark "open my map" in a contemporary high-contrast editorial serif, compact but airy, lower-case, single line centered. Large wordmark is the entire hero. The first o is a nearly circular thick-thin ring with a tiny precisely cut opening at its upper right, subtly evoking "open". Give the p descender a beautiful long understated curved exit terminal that suggests the beginning of a walking route, staying close to the text. Other letters remain exceptionally legible. Black ink, one tiny vermilion square precisely at the end of that terminal. Typographic intelligence and one subtle intervention, NOT swashes or ornate calligraphy. Restrained contemporary arts publisher identity; sophisticated soft curves, custom ligature between m and y only if readable. No separate symbol above the wordmark. Small text is NOT desired; the wordmark must be large enough to see beautiful details.
