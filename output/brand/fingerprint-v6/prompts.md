# Open My Map — Natural fingerprint v6

基于用户希望更像真实指纹的反馈，探索自然捺印、精细扫描、油墨按压三种质感。使用内置 image_gen 生成合成指纹设计，非采集的个人指纹。PNG 概念稿，暖白负形尚未处理为透明底；未进行小尺寸适配。

## 19 自然捺印 · 河谷

文件：`19-natural-ink-river.png`

### Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create an artistic fingerprint-and-geography identity concept for "Open My Map".
Critical new user feedback: previous generated fingerprint logos looked like perfectly spaced concentric vector contour lines. The user now wants the tactile irregular REAL FINGERPRINT feeling. This is the primary requirement. The fingerprint must look like a believable human ink fingerprint impression captured at high resolution, NOT a geometrically constructed fingerprint icon, NOT topographic contour rings, NOT vector smooth spirals.
Anatomy: natural elliptical fingertip impression, slightly tilted, genuine loop/whorl ridge flow with a coherent core and delta, nonuniform ridge spacing, natural ridge bifurcations, short ending ridges, tiny islands and enclosures, uneven rounded ridge edges, subtly broken ink at individual pores, tiny variations in pressure and density. About 35-50 fine ridges across the widest region, varied naturally. Strong believable friction-ridge anatomy. No giant circular bullseye spiral. The entire impression should feel personally made by pressing an inked fingertip, with a gently irregular feathered perimeter. The object is the fingerprint IMPRESSION ONLY, no finger, no hand or skin photo.
Preserve the previous specific concept: geographic forms appear as intentional warm-ivory negative-space CUTOUTS that interrupt the actual fingerprint ink ridges, not an illustration pasted over the print. Geographic cutout shape is clean enough to read while the surrounding fingerprint retains its authentic grain and organic ridge structure. Geography occupies only about 20-25% of the impression. Prioritize visual unity and believable impression.
Presentation: one large central fingerprint emblem with tiny refined exact wordmark "Open My Map" below. Warm off-white plain background, no physical paper photographed, no shadows, no glossy finish, no mockup, no labels or extraneous text. Landscape 3:2 canvas, generous margins. High resolution, meticulous tactile ridge detail. A premium artful personal cartography identity. This is a stylized synthetic fingerprint, not a reproduction of a specific person's print.
Version A: classic dark charcoal-black ink fingerprint impression, moderately firm thumb pressure, rich and slightly uneven organic ink, subtle ridge pores and a few natural tiny gaps. Fingerprint has a naturally asymmetric loop core in lower middle and a clear delta on one side. Main cutout: one graceful winding warm-ivory river entering upper right and exiting lower left, only one main bend and a tiny branch; narrow enough to retain fingerprint structure. No extra sun, mountain or colored point. Bottom serif wordmark black. Design should feel like an actual ink fingerprint transformed by a single intelligent geographic cut, beautiful, understated and tactile. Avoid over-distressing or dirty paper.
