# Open My Map — Fingerprint geography v5

指纹为主体，通过纹路镂空展现地理要素的六个方向。使用内置 image_gen 生成，交付 PNG 概念稿；未矢量化或做小尺寸适配。图中的暖白区域用于表现负形，不是透明通道。

## 13 指纹 · 河谷

文件：`13-fingerprint-river.png`

### Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Design an original artistic logo for "Open My Map", a personal map creation and sharing product.
User's specific concept: a FINGERPRINT whose ridge pattern is CUT AWAY using NEGATIVE SPACE to reveal recognizable geographic elements. The fingerprint is the primary unmistakable subject; geography must be literally carved out of the fingerprint as clean ivory voids, NOT drawn as an illustration pasted on top and NOT enclosed in a generic fingerprint-shaped frame. Visually this is "landscape revealed through a fingerprint". Refined high-end graphic identity, clever figure-ground integration, intentional optical balance.
Use an authentic stylized upright oval fingerprint silhouette with a clearly readable off-center whorl and sweeping arch ridges wrapping around it. Approximately 16-22 smooth substantial ridge lines, consistent medium stroke and good gaps, rounded ridge terminals, strong clear pattern, not ultra-thin hairlines. Keep at least 65% of the fingerprint visibly intact to preserve recognition. The geographic cutouts interrupt selected ridges; ivory background flows through the silhouette where appropriate. Flat two-tone artwork, vector-like precise clean edges, no shading, no gradients, no texture, no 3D.
Presentation: landscape 3:2 warm ivory canvas. ONE centered logo emblem, about 58% canvas height, refined small wordmark "Open My Map" centered below with ample spacing, understated typography. Wide calm margins. One design only. No mockup, no multiple variants, no board, no extra text, no watermarks. No hand, no actual finger photo, no forensic or biometric scanner UI, no padlock, no location pin, no random dots. Emblem must feel like a memorable crafted logo rather than a busy illustration.

GEOGRAPHY CUTOUT: a single broad sinuous river in ivory negative space winds diagonally through the fingerprint from upper right to lower left. Give the river one elegant narrowing bend near center and one tiny side tributary, no more. Ridge lines on both riverbanks continue with coherent fingerprint flow, so at first glance a fingerprint, second glance a river valley. A tiny flat burnt-orange solid circle sits on one bank near center as the one chosen place, small and restrained. Very dark forest green ridge ink. Wordmark in dark green refined humanist sans. Distinctive geographic integration; river is a void removing ridges, not a colored stripe. A premium thoughtful cartography logo.
