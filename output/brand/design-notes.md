Make the silhouette uniquely ownable, balanced and beautifully proportioned, with generous negative space, softly controlled corners, and confident diagonal fold lines. A flat, nearly single-color rich indigo blue with one subtly differentiated brighter blue plane, no gradients. Elegant Swiss identity design with a warm approachable contemporary digital product feeling. Recognition at favicon sizes is important: no thin lines or intricate details.

Avoid: generic teardrop location pins, Google Maps visual identity or Google multicolor, globe clipart, stock icon style, compass, complicated illustration, multiple concepts, shadows, bevels, 3D rendering, texture, mockups, gradients, watermarks.
Deliver a crisp polished logo mark ready to be placed into a website header.
